# Changelog

## 0.1.0-rc.1 — 2026-11-17 (Pre-release)

- Public C5 source and English/Japanese documentation for native v9.1.
- Reproducible build without private owner Flash; complete source and notices.
- Portable Windows setup/menu, matching PC controller and read-only backup,
  bootloader qualification and before/after installation audit tools.
- 320×172 JPEG preview, 180° physical LCD rotation, brightness and PC controls.
- Correct Cortex-M4F `fpv4-sp-d16` build; unsupported-instruction rejection and
  stacked PC/LR fault diagnostics from native v9.1.
- Owner confirmed v9.1 standalone play, PC controls, preview/enlarge/save,
  physical flip and brightness on one C5; live HID/JPEG reception recorded.
- Follow-up: owner restored stock V8.5 on the same C5 and tried its functions
  without observing problems. This is a functional report, not a full Flash audit.
- Long-duration stability, a new full Flash preservation audit and other
  units/bootloaders remain unverified.
